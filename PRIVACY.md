# Privacy Policy — GS AI Keyboard & GS AI Flow

**Last updated: 10 July 2026**

GS AI Keyboard (Android) and GS AI Flow (Windows) are voice-typing and smart-keyboard apps
made by Gobinda Sarkar. This policy explains, in plain language, what the apps do with your
data. The short version: **your data stays on your device, except the audio and text you
explicitly send to the AI provider you configured — using your own API key.**

## What the apps collect and why

### Microphone / voice recordings
When you start a dictation (tapping the mic, the Voice chip, the Flow bubble, or pressing the
hotkey on Windows), the app records audio from your microphone. That recording is sent
**directly from your device to the AI provider you configured yourself** (for example Google
Gemini, OpenAI, or another provider you added in Settings → Models), using **your own API
key**, solely to convert your speech to text. Recordings are not sent anywhere else, are not
stored on any server we operate (we don't operate any servers), and the temporary local audio
file is deleted after a successful transcription.

The AI provider processes that audio under **its own privacy policy and terms** — for
example, free-tier Google Gemini may use submitted content to improve its services. Choose
your provider (and its paid/free tier) accordingly, and avoid dictating sensitive personal or
financial details on free tiers.

### Text you type or rewrite
The keyboard's typing, prediction, and auto-correct features run **entirely on your device**.
Only when you explicitly use an AI feature (a rewrite chip like Fix/Professional/Flirty, the
Translate chip, or a voice command) is the relevant text sent to your configured AI provider,
again with your own key, to perform that one action.

### What is stored locally on your device only
- Your dictation **history** (so you can review/edit/reuse past dictations)
- Your **personal dictionary**, learned words, and word-pair predictions
- Your **snippets**, clipboard history, settings, and theme choices
- Your AI provider **API keys**

None of this ever leaves your device except as described above. Deleting the app deletes all
of it. You can also clear history, clipboard items, and learned words from inside the app at
any time.

### Accessibility Service (Android "Flow" mode, optional)
If you enable Flow mode and its Accessibility Service, the service is used **only** to detect
which text field currently has focus so the floating dictation bubble can appear next to it,
and to insert your transcribed text into that field. It does not read your screen content for
any other purpose, does not log what other apps display, and does nothing at all unless Flow
mode is switched on in Settings.

### Clipboard
The keyboard offers a clipboard history feature that stores copied text **locally** for your
own reuse. On Windows, text insertion works via a brief copy-paste that restores your
previous clipboard content immediately afterwards.

## What the apps do NOT do
- No ads, no trackers, no analytics SDKs
- No accounts — nothing to sign up for
- No data is sold, shared, or transferred to us or to any third party (other than the AI
  provider **you** configured, for the single request you initiated)
- No collection of contacts, location, files, or browsing history

## Data security
API keys and all local data are stored in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot
read. Network calls to AI providers are made over HTTPS.

## Children
These apps are general-purpose utilities not directed at children under 13.
